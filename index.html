SELECTED WORK

Case files

REC. 01
RESEARCH · GENDER · POLICY

Behind the Scenes — Rwanda

Youth Researcher, ICRW / University of Rwanda · 2024–2026

Co-designed and led a national mixed-methods study on women's pay, participation and career mobility in Rwanda's creative sector, funded by the Mastercard Foundation. Applied a gender-responsive framework across the full research lifecycle — instrument design, fieldwork, cleaning and analysis — using KoBo Toolbox, STATA, MAXQDA and Excel.

  • 400 survey respondents
  • 7 focus group discussions
  • 4 key informant interviews
  • 12 field researchers supervised

The resulting brief documents structural barriers women face — pay inequity, harassment and unsafe working conditions, unpaid care burdens, and exclusion from male-dominated production infrastructure — and sets out reforms for government, guilds, employers and development partners. I co-authored 30+ knowledge products in total and presented findings at national dissemination workshops.
